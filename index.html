The ladder

Eleven ranks, one road

The full World Taekwondo Federation syllabus with structured gradings. Every belt below is a real test, run on Monthly Grading Day.

Rank 01 of 11

White Belt

10th Gup · Beginner

Day one. No experience assumed and nothing to prove — the belt marks a start, not a level.

Swipe across the belt to change rank
Feature 01

Skill Level

Stance, guard and basic blocks. Learning to hold a position before learning to break one.

Feature 02

Poomsae

No form required yet. Attention goes to bowing, terminology and mat discipline.

Feature 03

Grading

Awarded on joining. The first real test comes at the end of your first cycle.

Syllabus follows the World Taekwondo / Kukkiwon gup system. Time on each belt depends on attendance and batch — confirm the current cycle with your coach or on WhatsApp.
