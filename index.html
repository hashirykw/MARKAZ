The Academy

Pakistan's most complete Taekwondo institute

Markaz Martial Arts Academy runs a complete World Taekwondo Federation syllabus alongside dedicated Kickboxing, Grappling and MMA training — led by Head Coach AbdulSamad Khan at Markaz-e-Khail-o-Saqafat, Gulberg Town.

Certified instruction, competition-level coaching and a belt system that actually means something. Students compete nationally and represent the academy at international events.
