First 90 days

From walking in to holding your own

1
Week 1

Trial & fit

You watch, then you train. No fee, no kit — clothes you can move in and water.

2
Weeks 2–4

Fundamentals

Stance, guard, base and breathing. The month that decides whether everything after works.

3
Weeks 5–8

Drilling live

Positional rounds with a coach watching. Resistance rises as fast as your control does.

4
Weeks 9–12

First grading

Controlled full rounds, then your first honest belt test.

OUJ Performance

OUJ carries its own identity and logo, but it is not a separate business — it sits under the same leadership as Markaz, handling the performance side of athlete development.

Where Markaz owns the syllabus, belts and technique, OUJ owns the engine: strength, conditioning, recovery and the gear that supports it.
